Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Heliora Consulting Limited ("Heliora", "we", "us") is a solar engineering consultancy based in Abuja, Nigeria. This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit helioraconsulting.com, send us an enquiry, or respond to one of our advertisements on LinkedIn, Facebook or Instagram, and what we do with it.

We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and the regulations issued under it. Where the data of people outside Nigeria is involved, we also apply the equivalent protections they are entitled to.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Heliora Consulting Limited is the data controller.

  • Address: Bassan Plaza, Central Business District, FCT Abuja, Nigeria
  • Email: contact@helioraconsulting.com

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you complete the enquiry form on our website, or a lead form on LinkedIn or Meta (Facebook and Instagram), we collect:

  • your name, email address and, if you provide them, phone or WhatsApp number and organisation;
  • your job title or role in the decision, where the form asks for it;
  • details about your project: the service you need, client type, project scale, stage, decision timeline and any message you write.

When you respond to a LinkedIn or Meta lead form, the platform pre-fills some fields from your profile. You can edit them before you submit, and only what you submit is shared with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, recorded with each enquiry to prevent spam and abuse.
  • Campaign data: the advertisement, campaign and placement that brought you to the site (UTM tags and click identifiers such as fbclid, gclid and li_fat_id).
  • Cookie data: cookie identifiers, the pages you view and the actions you take on the site, collected by the analytics and advertising tools listed in section 6.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the business, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Zoho, our customer relationship management (CRM) system, where enquiries are stored and followed up;
  • Namecheap, which hosts our website and enquiry database;
  • LinkedIn and Meta, which deliver our advertisements and lead forms and measure how they perform. When you send an enquiry through our website, we send Meta a confirmation that an enquiry was made, with your contact details hashed (scrambled one-way) so it can match the conversion to an advert without receiving them in readable form;
  • Google and X, for website analytics and advertising measurement.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, or to protect our rights in a dispute.

5. International transfers

Some of these providers store data outside Nigeria, including in the European Union and the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 allows, such as the provider's standard contractual commitments and the adequacy of the destination country's protection.

6. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses analytics and advertising tools to measure its performance: how many people visit, which pages they read, and which advertisements bring enquiries from project developers and funders. These tools load when you visit and set cookies on your device:

  • Google Analytics (Google), for visit and page statistics;
  • Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram), LinkedIn Insight Tag and X pixel, to measure which of our advertisements lead to visits and enquiries, and to show our advertisements to people who have visited the site.

We use this information only to run and improve our website and advertising. We do not sell your data.

You can limit or stop these tools at any time:

  • block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use your browser's private mode or a tracker blocker;
  • install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on;
  • change your advertising settings on Facebook, LinkedIn and X;
  • email contact@helioraconsulting.com to object, and we will stop sending conversion data about your enquiry to the advertising platforms.

Blocking these tools does not affect how the site or the enquiry form works.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and client records for as long as we are in contact with you, and for up to three years after our last contact, unless a longer period is required for tax, accounting or legal reasons, or for a contract we are performing. Technical logs are kept for a shorter time. When data is no longer needed, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you have the right to:

  • be told how your data is used, and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted, or its use restricted;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before;
  • receive your data in a portable format.

To use any of these rights, email contact@helioraconsulting.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (ndpc.gov.ng).

9. Security

Our website is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to enquiry records is limited to the staff who need it, and our CRM and hosting accounts are protected with individual logins. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses and public bodies. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact@helioraconsulting.com, or write to Heliora Consulting Limited, Bassan Plaza, Central Business District, FCT Abuja, Nigeria.
