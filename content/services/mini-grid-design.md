Mini-grid design is Heliora's core work. We engineer solar mini-grids for communities that the main grid does not reach, and for sites where the grid will arrive later. The design has to survive three reviews: the regulator's, the funder's and the site's. We design for all three from the first load survey.

## Who we work with

- **Mini-grid developers** preparing sites for a programme, a grant or project finance
- **Rural electrification agencies and programmes** that need consistent, reviewable designs across a portfolio of sites
- **Development partners and funders** who need an independent design or a second opinion on one
- **EPC contractors** who have won a mini-grid contract and need the detailed engineering done properly

## What we deliver

- **Demand assessment and load forecast.** A site survey of households, businesses, productive users and public facilities, turned into an hourly load profile with a growth assumption you can defend.
- **System sizing and modelling.** PV, battery and inverter sizing optimised in HOMER Pro, with PVsyst yield modelling and battery storage optimisation. Options are compared, not assumed.
- **Isolated mini-grid design.** Off-grid solar-battery systems for unserved communities, typically 50 kW to 1 MW.
- **Grid-interconnected design.** Hybrid mini-grids designed for later connection to the national grid, with the protection coordination that interconnection needs.
- **Detailed engineering package.** Single-line diagrams, protection relay settings, power house and civil drawings, distribution network layout and bills of materials, ready for tender and construction.
- **Regulatory and programme submissions.** Design documentation structured for the regulator in your country (NERC in Nigeria) and for programme or lender technical review.

## How an engagement runs

1. **Scoping.** We agree the sites, the programme or funder requirements, and the deliverables. You get a fixed-scope, fixed-fee proposal.
2. **Site data.** Load survey, site visit, solar resource data and any existing grid information.
3. **Concept and sizing.** Configuration options with costs and trade-offs, so you choose with the numbers in front of you.
4. **Detailed design.** The full engineering package, reviewed internally by a senior engineer before it leaves our desk.
5. **Tender and construction support.** Technical specifications for bidding, bid evaluation, and, if you want it, our [Owner's Engineer service](/insights/owners-engineer-solar-mini-grid/) through construction and commissioning.

## Isolated or interconnected?

An isolated mini-grid is sized to carry the community on its own, so battery autonomy and generator backup drive the design. An interconnected design assumes the national grid may arrive. The protection scheme, metering and network standards then have to allow a safe connection later, so the assets are not stranded. If grid extension is likely within the project's life, it pays to design for it now.

## Common questions

**Can you design to our programme's template?**
Yes. Most electrification programmes set their own formats for demand data, sizing reports and drawings. We structure deliverables to match them, which shortens the review.

**What do you need from us to start?**
The site list and coordinates, any survey data you already hold, and the programme or funder requirements. If there is no survey data, we collect it.

**Do you only work in Nigeria?**
We are based in Abuja and take on projects across Africa. Outside Nigeria we design to the regulator and grid code of the country concerned.
