# Google Search Ads: keyword and volume research

Heliora Consulting Limited · researched 9 November 2026 (two rounds) · Google Ads Keyword Planner (PROSOLAR account, read-only research)

**Target market:** Nigeria plus sub-Saharan Africa, **South Africa included** (decided 9 Nov 2026).

## Bottom line

Widening the keyword list beyond consulting terms roughly quadruples the reachable search traffic. That means adding rural electrification, off-grid, commercial and industrial (C&I) solar, energy efficiency and audits, EPC, engineering design, PPAs, captive power and battery storage.

| Keyword set | Terms | Best-value budget | Clicks / month | Avg. CPC | Enquiries (Google's estimate) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Round 1: consulting terms only | 54 | ~$7/day (~$220/mo) | ~87 | $2.57 | ~2 |
| **Round 2: business-buyer list (recommended)** | 62 | **$10–15/day (~$310–465/mo)** | **~220–280** | $1.40–$2 | ~6–8 |
| Round 2: broad list, including consumer terms | 77 | $10/day (~$310/mo) | ~670 | $0.46 | ~20 (inflated: many are home buyers) |

The **business-buyer list at $10–15 a day** is the best balance. Past about $30 a day, clicks barely grow while cost per click climbs.

Google's enquiry estimates use a generic conversion rate. For a specialist consultancy, expect fewer and better-qualified enquiries than that suggests.

## What this means

1. **The wider list works.** C&I solar, rural electrification, EPC, energy audits, ESIA, PPAs and battery storage add real business demand. Each term is small, but together they are about four times the consulting-only volume.
2. **South Africa leads, followed by Kenya, Nigeria and Uganda.** South Africa is about 60% of business-buyer clicks. Give it its own campaign and its own ad copy. It's a mature market, so lead with independence, bankability and Owner's Engineer work rather than "solar consultant".
3. **"Mini grid" and "rural electrification" are the biggest business terms, and both are mixed intent.** Use phrase match with a strong negative list (jobs, research, students, REA itself).
4. **Leave out product-buyer terms.** "Solar companies", "off grid solar system", "solar solutions" and similar cut the cost per click, but the people clicking want panels, not engineers.
5. **French-speaking markets need French keywords.** Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire barely register in English.

## Recommended setup

- **Account:** a Heliora Google Ads account, not PROSOLAR's. Set up the Google Ads conversion action and paste its ID into `googleAds` in `index.html`.
- **Budget:** $10–15 a day in total to start, split roughly South Africa 50% · Nigeria 25% · East Africa (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda) 20% · rest 5%.
- **Bidding:** pay per click. Start with Maximize clicks and a max CPC of about $3, then switch to Maximize conversions after 15–30 enquiries.
- **Campaigns:** (1) South Africa · (2) Nigeria · (3) Rest of English-speaking sub-Saharan Africa.
- **Ad groups, one per service, each with its own landing page:**
  1. Owner's Engineer and project management
  2. Feasibility studies and technical due diligence
  3. Mini-grid and rural electrification
  4. C&I solar (commercial, industrial, factories, hotels, telecom, diesel hybrid, captive power, PPA)
  5. Solar engineering design and EPC support
  6. Energy efficiency and energy audits
  7. ESIA
  8. Battery storage (BESS)
  9. General solar / renewable energy consultant
- **Match types:** phrase and exact only.
- **Starter negative keywords:** jobs, job, vacancy, careers, salary, recruitment, internship, course, training, pdf, thesis, project report, wikipedia, definition, meaning, price, cost of panels, panel, inverter, battery price, kit, home, house, residential, DIY, near me (test it before excluding), agency (for REA name searches), McKinsey, Deloitte, Accenture, KPMG, EY, PwC, BCG, oil and gas, offshore wind, irrigation, pump.
- **Landing pages** (built 9 Nov 2026):
  - Mini-grid and rural electrification → `/services/mini-grid-design/`
  - Energy efficiency and audits → `/services/energy-audits/`
  - ESIA → `/services/esia/`
  - Battery storage → `/insights/battery-storage-sizing/`
  - Owner's Engineer → `/insights/owners-engineer-solar-mini-grid/`
  - Feasibility → `/insights/bankable-solar-feasibility-study/`
  - C&I solar → `/insights/ci-solar-replacing-diesel/`
  - General consultant terms → home page
